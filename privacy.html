Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28.07.2026

Suuth is designed to be a quiet daily companion for hydration and gentle self-care.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Suuth uses, what stays on your device, what limited information is processed outside the app, and what choices you have.

1. What Suuth is

Suuth helps you track water and drink intake, monitor your hydration progress, and set gentle reminders.

Some drink options and related features may require Suuth Premium.

Suuth is a general wellness app. It is not a medical app and is not a replacement for advice from a doctor or qualified health professional.

2. Information stored on your device

Suuth does not require you to create an account.

The information you enter into Suuth is stored locally on your device.

This may include:

  • water and drink entries
  • drink amounts and drink types
  • daily hydration goals
  • hydration history and progress
  • reminder settings and selected reminder days
  • wake-up and bedtime preferences
  • selected units and appearance preferences
  • premium access status
  • profile name, icon, and profile color
  • age, gender, height, and weight you choose to add
  • activity level, climate, and wellness goal selections

Suuth does not use a Suuth cloud account system to store this information.

3. Apple Health

Suuth can connect with Apple Health if you give permission.

Apple Health access is optional. Suuth still works if you do not grant access.

With your permission, Suuth may read:

  • date of birth or age
  • biological sex
  • height
  • weight

Suuth uses this information only inside the app to help suggest a more personal daily hydration goal.

With your permission, Suuth may write water intake to Apple Health.

If you track another drink, Suuth may calculate a water-equivalent hydration amount and, with your permission, write that amount to Apple Health as water intake.

You can change Apple Health permissions at any time in the Health app or iOS Settings.

Suuth does not sell Apple Health information.

Suuth does not use Apple Health information for advertising, marketing, analytics, or tracking you across other apps or websites.

Information obtained from Apple Health is not sent to Mixpanel or any other third-party analytics service.

4. Notifications

Suuth may send hydration reminders if you turn reminders on.

These reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. They are not sent from a Suuth notification server.

Your reminder schedule, selected reminder days, wake-up time, bedtime, and notification permission status are not sent to the analytics service.

You can change or turn off reminders at any time in Suuth Settings or iOS Settings.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

Suuth Premium purchases are processed through Apple’s App Store purchase system.

Suuth does not collect or store your payment card or bank account details.

Apple may process information related to purchases, subscriptions, renewals, cancellations, and refunds under Apple’s own terms and privacy practices.

6. Limited product analytics

Suuth uses Mixpanel to collect limited product-interaction analytics. This helps us understand whether important parts of the app are working and which areas may need improvement.

The analytics may include:

  • app launches and session count
  • screens opened inside Suuth
  • onboarding screens viewed
  • settings sections and links selected
  • paywall views, closures, and successful purchases
  • subscription status and subscription type
  • appearance preference
  • the type of add-drink flow used, such as quick add
  • the selected statistics period
  • basic technical metadata supplied by the analytics service, such as app version and device or operating-system information

The analytics system uses a randomly generated installation identifier. It is not based on your name, email address, phone number, Apple Health information, or advertising identifier.

Automatic event collection is disabled. Suuth defines its product events manually.

IP-address-based geolocation is disabled in Suuth’s Mixpanel configuration.

Session replay is not used.

Suuth does not use analytics for advertising, data brokerage, or tracking you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Suuth does not send the following information to Mixpanel:

  • your name, age, gender, height, or weight
  • activity level, climate, or wellness goal
  • daily hydration goal or hydration progress
  • drink amounts, drink identity, or hydration history
  • streak information
  • reminder schedules, wake-up time, or bedtime
  • notification permission status
  • Apple Health permission status or Apple Health data

7. Ads, sale, and cross-app tracking

Suuth does not show third-party advertisements.

Suuth does not use your information to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Suuth does not sell personal information.

8. Support communications

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you include in your message.

We use this information to answer your question, provide support, or respond to feedback.

Please avoid including sensitive health information in support emails unless it is necessary for your request.

9. Data deletion

You may delete individual hydration entries inside Suuth where this option is available.

You may also delete your hydration history or other local app data from Suuth Settings.

If Apple Health is connected, Suuth will also try to remove matching hydration entries that Suuth previously added to Apple Health.

Deleting Suuth from your device generally removes its local app data.

Information already stored separately in Apple Health is controlled through Apple Health.

You may contact us to ask questions about analytics information or request deletion of analytics information associated with your installation.

We may need reasonable information to locate the relevant installation data.

10. Data retention and security

Local hydration and preference information remains on your device until you delete it, delete the related entry, reset the app’s local data, or remove the app.

Mixpanel and Apple may retain limited information according to their own retention policies and Suuth’s service configuration.

We take reasonable steps to limit the information collected and to use services that provide privacy and security controls.

No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. International processing

Third-party service providers may process information in countries different from the country where you live.

Suuth sends its Mixpanel analytics events to Mixpanel’s European data-processing endpoint.

12. Children’s privacy

Suuth is not intended for children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us so we can review the situation.

13. Your choices

You can:

  • use Suuth without connecting Apple Health
  • change Apple Health permissions in the Health app or iOS Settings
  • turn reminders off
  • delete individual hydration entries
  • delete local Suuth data
  • cancel Suuth Premium through your Apple Account settings
  • contact us with a privacy or deletion request

14. Third-party services

Suuth currently uses the following third-party services:

  • Mixpanel for limited product-interaction analytics
  • Apple App Store services for purchases and subscriptions
  • Apple Health, when you choose to connect it

These services process information according to their own applicable privacy and data-processing terms.

15. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Suuth changes.

If we make meaningful changes, we will update the effective date and make the revised policy available through Suuth or its public website.
