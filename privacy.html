Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28.07.2026

Suuth is designed to be a quiet daily companion for hydration and gentle self-care.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Suuth uses, what stays on your device, what limited information may be processed outside the app, and what choices you have.

1. What Suuth does

Suuth helps you track water and other drinks, follow your hydration progress, and set gentle reminders.

Some drink options and related features may require Suuth Premium.

Suuth is a general wellness app. It is not a medical app and does not replace advice from a doctor or other qualified health professional.

2. Information stored on your device

You do not need to create an account to use Suuth.

The information you enter into Suuth is stored locally on your device.

This may include:

  • water and drink entries
  • drink amounts and drink types
  • daily hydration goals
  • hydration history and progress
  • reminder settings and selected reminder days
  • wake-up and bedtime preferences
  • units and appearance preferences
  • premium access status
  • profile name, icon, and profile color
  • age, gender, height, and weight you choose to add
  • activity level, climate, and wellness goal selections

Suuth does not use a Suuth cloud account to store this information in this version.

3. Apple Health

Suuth can connect with Apple Health if you give permission.

Apple Health access is optional. Suuth still works if you do not connect it.

With your permission, Suuth may read:

  • date of birth or age
  • biological sex
  • height
  • weight

Suuth uses this information inside the app to help suggest a more personal daily hydration goal.

With your permission, Suuth may also write water intake to Apple Health.

When you track another drink, Suuth may calculate a water-equivalent hydration amount and write that amount to Apple Health as water intake.

You can change Apple Health permissions at any time in the Health app or iOS Settings.

Suuth does not sell Apple Health information.

Suuth does not use Apple Health information for advertising, marketing, analytics, or tracking across other apps or websites.

Apple Health information is not sent to any analytics service.

4. Notifications

Suuth may send hydration reminders if you turn reminders on.

These are local notifications scheduled on your device. They are not sent from a Suuth notification server.

You can change or turn off reminders at any time in Suuth Settings or iOS Settings.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

Suuth Premium purchases are handled through Apple’s App Store purchase system.

Suuth does not collect or store your payment card or bank account details.

Apple may process information related to purchases, subscriptions, renewals, cancellations, and refunds under Apple’s own terms and privacy practices.

6. Limited product analytics

Suuth uses Mixpanel for limited product analytics.

This helps us understand how people move through the app, whether important features are working, and which areas may need improvement.

This may include general app activity, screen activity, interactions with onboarding, settings, statistics, drink-entry flows, and the Suuth Premium purchase experience, along with subscription status and basic app or device information.

The analytics system uses a randomly generated installation identifier. It is not based on your name, email address, phone number, Apple Health information, or advertising identifier.

Automatic event collection is disabled. Suuth defines its analytics events manually.

IP-based location tracking is disabled in Suuth’s Mixpanel setup.

Session replay is not used.

Suuth does not use analytics for advertising, data brokerage, or tracking across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Suuth does not send profile details, hydration entries, drink amounts, reminder schedules, or Apple Health information to Mixpanel.

7. Ads, sale, and tracking

Suuth does not show third-party advertisements.

Suuth does not use your information to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Suuth does not sell personal information.

8. Support messages

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you include in your message.

We use this information to answer your question, provide support, or respond to feedback.

Please avoid including sensitive health information in support emails unless it is necessary for your request.

9. Data deletion

You may delete individual hydration entries inside Suuth where this option is available.

You may also delete your hydration history from Suuth Settings.

If Apple Health is connected, Suuth will also try to remove matching hydration entries that Suuth previously added to Apple Health.

Deleting Suuth from your device generally removes its local app data.

Information already stored separately in Apple Health is managed through Apple Health.

You may contact us with questions about analytics information or to request deletion of analytics information associated with your installation.

We may need reasonable information to locate the relevant installation data.

10. Data retention and security

Local hydration and preference information remains on your device until you delete it, delete the related entry, reset the app’s local data, or remove the app.

Mixpanel and Apple may retain limited information according to their own retention policies and Suuth’s service settings.

We take reasonable steps to limit the information collected and to use services that provide privacy and security controls.

No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. International processing

Third-party service providers may process information in countries different from the country where you live.

Suuth sends Mixpanel analytics events to Mixpanel’s European data-processing endpoint.

12. Children’s privacy

Suuth is not intended for children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us so we can review the situation.

13. Your choices

You can:

  • use Suuth without connecting Apple Health
  • change Apple Health permissions in the Health app or iOS Settings
  • turn reminders off
  • delete individual hydration entries
  • delete your hydration history
  • cancel Suuth Premium through your Apple Account settings
  • contact us with a privacy or deletion request

14. Third-party services

Suuth currently uses:

  • Mixpanel for limited product analytics
  • Apple App Store services for purchases and subscriptions
  • Apple Health, when you choose to connect it

These services process information according to their own privacy and data-processing terms.

15. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Suuth changes.

When we make meaningful changes, we will update the effective date and make the revised policy available through Suuth or its public website.
